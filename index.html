Video tour study

Help us compare methods that turn a few photos of a landmark into a video tour that flies around it.

What you will do

  1. Each trial shows a starting photo, a few other photos of the same place taken at other times, and two videos side by side. Both videos start from the starting photo and fly the same camera path.
  2. Watch both videos to the end. The answer buttons unlock after one full play.
  3. Answer by picking the left or the right video. The first question asks which video you would prefer for a virtual tour of the place; three shorter questions follow, on the look, the place and the quality. There are no right answers; go with your impression. If the two look equally good, pick the one you lean toward.

About 22 trials, about 13 minutes. Please use a laptop or desktop screen with the browser window as large as possible, and do not resize it during the study.

Your answers

Your answers are anonymous. We record your choices, how long each trial took, your window size and the optional answer below. We do not record your name, email or address.

Your window is narrow. The videos are shown side by side and need a laptop or desktop screen.

The questions

    The starting photo sets the look: the video should keep its light, colours, weather and time of day. The other photos were taken at other times, so their look can differ; use them to see what the place really looks like (its buildings and shapes).
