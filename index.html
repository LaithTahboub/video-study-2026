Video tour study

Help us compare computer programs that turn a few photos of a famous place into a video tour of it.

What you will do

  1. Each trial shows a starting photo, a few other photos of the same place, and two videos side by side. Both videos begin at the starting photo and move along the same camera path.
  2. Watch both videos. They repeat by themselves, and you can watch them as many times as you like.
  3. For each question, pick the left or the right video, or Same if you really cannot tell them apart. There are no right or wrong answers, so go with your first impression.

About 22 trials, about 13 minutes. Please use a laptop or desktop computer, make the browser window as large as possible, and keep it that size during the study.

Your answers

Your answers are anonymous. We record your choices, how long each trial took, your window size and the optional answer below. We do not record your name, email or address.

Your window is narrow. The videos are shown side by side and need a laptop or desktop screen.

The questions

    Important: judge appearance (lighting, colors, weather, time of day) against the starting photo.

    Important: judge the place (buildings, shapes, details) against the closest photo, which changes as the videos play.
